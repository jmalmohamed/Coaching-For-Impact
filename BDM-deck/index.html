Part 1 · Why

01 · The client problem

Training and tools fade when nobody follows up. Coaching in the routines makes them hold.

Clients judge dss+ on results that last after we leave. When leaders are coached inside the routines they already run, results move within months.

Indicative results · dss+ Area Transformation programmes
For the client

For dss+

Our results are judged after we leave. If practice fades, the programme is remembered as "a report and some workshops".

What coaching adds

Someone works with leaders on applying the change in their own work, week after week, until it holds without us.

Part 3 · When to sell

07 · Prerequisites

No prerequisites, no coaching.

These nine conditions decide whether coaching will succeed. They are also what the client commits to. Score the opportunity: four of them are critical.

Walk away when
  • The gap is only knowledge: sell training (coaching can follow)
  • The gap is a broken system or missing resources: sell consulting first
  • Coaching is meant to "fix" a person, as a disciplinary step
  • The timeline is under 3 months
  • Coaching is only a box to tick in a bid

Part 5 · How to shape it

13 · Modalities

Choose the modality by who is coached and why.

Three modalities, delivered face to face, online, or a mix. Most programmes combine them, as Project Falcon does. Keep sessions no more than 2 weeks apart where you can.

Rotating crews

For 14/14 or 28/28 rotations, plan sessions around each hitch on site and use short online sessions to keep the rhythm while the leader is away.

Project Falcon mix (illustrative)

One-to-one for 8 area superintendents · 24 supervisors in 8 groups of 3 · team coaching for the plant leadership team · online check-ins between field sessions · 4 internal coaches developed for handover.

How the estimate works
  • Coaching days = the larger of: total hours (session + prep) ÷ hours per coach day, or sessions ÷ max sessions per coach day. Travel and shift times limit how many sessions fit in a day.
  • Design = base + days per linked workstream + days per leader group.
  • Measurement setup = base + days per coaching outcome + days per site.
  • Reporting = months × days per month: monthly progress report and steering input.
  • Internal coaches = coaches × days each: training, co-coaching and quality checks.

Rules
  • Never cut line manager meetings or measurement to save cost
  • Levers that lower cost: fewer sessions for experienced leaders, online check-ins, internal coaches for handover, a pilot before scaling

Parameters are planning assumptions for this playbook, not dss+ standard rates. Check them with the coaching lead before you price.
